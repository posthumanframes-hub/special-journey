Notes on this site

Why does my site look like this?

The tiles on this site are drawn from the hand-made cement floors of Athangudi, a village in the Chettinad region of Tamil Nadu.

The motifs here

Each project has its own tile, redrawn from traditional patterns.

  • SampangiChampak flower
  • KolamThreshold drawing
  • NakshatraStar
  • MaangaMango
  • ThamaraiLotus
  • MalliJasmine
  • JaaliLattice screen
  • ChakramWheel
  • GopuramTemple tower
  • ValaivuArcs

Why I chose them

When I started making this site, I wanted a way to show my work that felt like mine. I kept coming back to Athangudi tiles.

A tile is a small thing. It is made by hand, one at a time.12 On its own, it is simple. But place it next to another, and a pattern begins to show.

That is how my work feels to me. Some projects fit closely together, like a finished floor. You’ll find them in Work. Others are looser, still finding their place. Those are in Wander.

And a tile is never just a tile. It carries a place, a material and the hands that made it. That is what I’m curious about: how the things we make carry the people and places around them.
